How I got here

MCA final-year student at Loyola Academy. I build websites with AI for speed — then check every pixel myself, on a real phone and a real desktop, before anything ships.

Most websites are built to look finished. Mine are built to work finished. A site isn’t done when the code compiles — it’s done when a stranger can open it on their phone and get it in five seconds.

I move fast because AI does the heavy lifting. But speed without taste is just noise, so every layout, word, and interaction gets a human pass. Built with AI, finished by hand.

And I don’t ship surprises. Every site is verified live — phone and desktop — before delivery. If it doesn’t impress on your screen, it doesn’t leave mine.

Why Merugu Studio?

Because you don’t need an agency. You need one person who answers fast, builds fast, and treats your site like his own portfolio piece. That’s the whole business model.
